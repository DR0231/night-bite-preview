Boating on Hoover 10 hp, 10 mph
  • Boats up to 10 hp, 8 to 22 ft long (sailboats up to 25 ft). Rec and Parks lists the max as 9.9 hp.
  • No motorized boat over 10 mph, and idle speed within 100 ft of shore.
  • A motor over 10 hp has to stay tilted up or have its prop removed.
  • No water skiing and no personal watercraft. Rec and Parks also lists no wakeboarding or tubing.
  • No boating within 100 ft downriver of the dam.
  • No swimming in any City of Columbus reservoir.
