        How the bite is rated

        The Bite is this site’s own rule of thumb, not fishing science and not a forecast. It adds points from readings already on this page.

        • Barometer, the change over the last 3 hours at the lake’s NWS station. Falling slowly +2, steady +1, rising slowly 0, falling fast 0, rising fast −1. Steady means within 0.02 inHg; fast means 0.06 inHg or more.
        • Wind, tonight’s average NWS hourly speed from sunset to 2 AM (the top of each hour’s range). Under 3 mph 0, 3 to 12 mph +1, 13 to 18 mph 0, over 18 mph −2.
        • Moon, worked out on this phone. New or full moon tonight (or under 10% or over 90% lit) +1, otherwise 0.
        • Water temp, from this lake’s USGS gauge where one reports it. 55 to 80°F +1, colder than 45 or warmer than 86°F −1, anything between 0. It never makes a rating alone.
        • Thunder anywhere in tonight’s NWS forecast makes it Tough, whatever the points.

        4 points or more is Good, 2 or 3 is Fair, less is Tough. A missing reading counts 0, and with no barometer trend and no wind there is no rating.
