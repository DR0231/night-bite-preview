About & disclaimer

The Night Bite is a personal, unofficial central Ohio night fishing hub with a night-dock look, covering three lakes: Hoover Reservoir, Alum Creek Lake and Buckeye Lake. Species pages, water maps, trip log, and gear for each one; pick a lake with Choose a lake at the top of any page. Night is especially useful for catfish (channel and flathead, plus blues at Hoover); it is not the best approach for every species.

Not official

  • Not the Ohio Department of Natural Resources (ODNR).
  • Not the City of Columbus.
  • Not USGS and not the US Army Corps of Engineers. Gauge snapshots are provisional and labeled; optional refresh never invents numbers.
  • Depth contours linked from Water are ODNR fishing maps: Hoover 2001 / 894-ft pool, Alum Creek 2001 / 888-ft pool, Buckeye 1998 / 891.75-ft pool. For reference only and not for navigation. No Garmin/Navionics.
  • Example log trips are fiction for layout demos, marked Example.
  • Tournaments listed only when submitted. This site is not a tournament director.

Hub scope

  • Three central Ohio lakes: Hoover Reservoir, Alum Creek Lake and Buckeye Lake. Hoover is the default.
  • Public access names only: no secret GPS, no private property tips.
  • Each lake keeps its own boating and safety notes on Water, gear, and species pages: 10 hp at Hoover, unlimited horsepower on Alum Creek’s south main lake with no wake on the north end, no wake within 300 ft of any land mass at Buckeye.

Where the numbers come from

  • Hoover: USGS gauge 03228400 for pool level and water temperature.
  • Alum Creek: US Army Corps of Engineers lake level gauge. No public water temperature gauge, so none is shown.
  • Buckeye: USGS lake gauge 395540082291600 for level only. No public water temperature gauge, so none is shown.
  • Forecast, barometer and alerts for each lake come from the National Weather Service. All gauge readings are provisional.

Safety

Fishing after dark can be dangerous. Storms, lightning, heat, cold, slick ramps, and low visibility are real risks. Turn back when weather turns. Use lights, tell someone your plan, and wear a life jacket when appropriate. Hoover and Alum Creek are drinking-water reservoirs. At every lake, pack out trash and follow posted rules.

License

An Ohio fishing license is required for anglers 16 and older (confirm current rules before you go). Buy and manage licenses through official ODNR / HuntFish OH channels, not through this site.

ODNR licenses / HuntFish OH · mention the HuntFish OH app for phone-side regs checks.

Regs reminders (verify 2026–27)

Plain-language reminders from the statewide inland pattern in ODNR Fishing Regulations 2026–27 (verify current). Confirm on HuntFish OH before you keep fish. Some waters differ (e.g. the crappie 9″ rule at all three lakes).

  • Channel cat: under 28″ no daily limit; 28″+ daily 1
  • Blue cat: under 35″ no daily limit; 35″+ daily 1
  • Flathead: under 35″ no daily limit; 35″+ daily 1
  • Largemouth / smallmouth / spotted: 5 combined, 12″ min
  • Striped / hybrid striped / white bass: 30 combined; no more than 4 over 15″
  • Walleye / sauger / saugeye: 6 combined; no minimum size at Hoover, Alum Creek or Buckeye (a 15″ minimum applies only on six other lakes)
  • Crappie at all three lakes: 30 a day, black and white combined, 9″ minimum (site-specific rule for Hoover, Alum Creek and Buckeye). ODNR has proposed dropping the 9″ minimum at Hoover from March 1, 2027
  • Muskellunge (Alum Creek): 1 a day, statewide rule; no minimum length listed statewide
  • Yellow perch (Buckeye): 30 a day, statewide rule
  • Bluegill / sunfish, carp, gar, bullhead: not on the statewide limits table, and none of the three lakes has a site-specific rule for them
