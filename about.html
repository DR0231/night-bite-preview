About & disclaimer

The Night Bite is a personal, unofficial central Ohio night fishing hub with a night-dock look, covering 11 lakes: Hoover Reservoir, Alum Creek Lake, Buckeye Lake, O’Shaughnessy Reservoir, Griggs Reservoir, Indian Lake, Delaware Lake, Deer Creek Lake, Madison Lake, Knox Lake and Clear Fork Reservoir. Species pages, water maps, trip log, and gear for each one; pick a lake with Choose a lake at the top of any page. Night is especially useful for catfish (channel and flathead, plus blues at Hoover); it is not the best approach for every species.

Hub scope

  • 11 central Ohio lakes: Hoover Reservoir, Alum Creek Lake, Buckeye Lake, O’Shaughnessy Reservoir, Griggs Reservoir, Indian Lake, Delaware Lake, Deer Creek Lake, Madison Lake, Knox Lake and Clear Fork Reservoir. Hoover is the default.
  • Public access names only: no secret GPS, no private property tips.
  • Each lake keeps its own boating and safety notes on Water, gear, and species pages: 10 hp at Hoover, unlimited horsepower on Alum Creek’s south main lake with no wake on the north end, no wake within 300 ft of any land mass at Buckeye Lake, unlimited horsepower with a 40 mph limit and no jet skis at the City’s O’Shaughnessy and Griggs reservoirs, no horsepower limit at Indian Lake, Delaware Lake or Deer Creek Lake, electric motors only at Madison Lake, 10 hp at Knox Lake (larger motors at no wake), and no wake on all of Clear Fork Reservoir with no horsepower limit (City of Mansfield).

Where the numbers come from

  • Hoover: USGS gauge 03228400 for pool level and water temperature.
  • Alum Creek: US Army Corps of Engineers lake level gauge. No public water temperature gauge, so none is shown.
  • Buckeye Lake: USGS lake gauge 395540082291600 for level only. No public water temperature gauge, so none is shown.
  • O’Shaughnessy: USGS gauge 03220500 for level only. The City says reservoir temperatures are not monitored, so none is shown.
  • Griggs: USGS gauge 03221500 for pool level and water temperature.
  • Indian Lake: USGS gauge 03260500 for lake level and water temperature.
  • Delaware Lake: US Army Corps of Engineers lake level gauge. No public water temperature gauge, so none is shown.
  • Deer Creek Lake: US Army Corps of Engineers lake level gauge. No public water temperature gauge (the Corps posts an outflow temperature only), so none is shown.
  • Madison Lake, Knox Lake and Clear Fork Reservoir: no public USGS or Corps lake level or water temperature gauge, so neither is shown.
  • Forecast, barometer and alerts for each lake come from the National Weather Service. All gauge readings are provisional.

Safety

Fishing after dark can be dangerous. Storms, lightning, heat, cold, slick ramps, and low visibility are real risks. Turn back when weather turns. Use lights, tell someone your plan, and wear a life jacket when appropriate. Hoover, Alum Creek, O’Shaughnessy, Griggs and Clear Fork are drinking-water reservoirs. At every lake, pack out trash and follow posted rules.

License

An Ohio fishing license is required for anglers 16 and older (confirm current rules before you go). Buy and manage licenses through official ODNR / HuntFish OH channels, not through this site.

ODNR licenses / HuntFish OH · mention the HuntFish OH app for phone-side regs checks.

Regs reminders (verify 2026–27)

Plain-language reminders from the statewide inland pattern in ODNR Fishing Regulations 2026–27 (verify current). Confirm on HuntFish OH before you keep fish. Some waters differ (e.g. the crappie 9″ rule at 7 of the 11 lakes, and an 18″ bass minimum at Knox Lake).

  • Channel cat: under 28″ no daily limit; 28″+ daily 1
  • Blue cat: under 35″ no daily limit; 35″+ daily 1
  • Flathead: under 35″ no daily limit; 35″+ daily 1
  • Largemouth / smallmouth / spotted: 5 combined, 12″ min; at Knox Lake 5 combined, 18″ min (site-specific rule)
  • Striped / hybrid striped / white bass: 30 combined; no more than 4 over 15″
  • Paddlefish: none may be taken within 2,000 ft of the Deer Creek dam (site-specific rule)
  • Walleye / sauger / saugeye: 6 combined; no minimum size at any of the 11 lakes (a 15″ minimum applies only on six other lakes)
  • Crappie at 7 of the 11 lakes: 30 a day, black and white combined, 9″ minimum (site-specific rule for Hoover, Alum Creek, Buckeye Lake, O’Shaughnessy, Indian, Delaware and Deer Creek). Griggs, Madison, Knox and Clear Fork are not on that list, so crappie there have no daily limit or minimum. ODNR has proposed dropping the 9″ minimum at Hoover from March 1, 2027
  • Muskellunge (Alum Creek, Clear Fork): 1 a day, statewide rule; no minimum length listed statewide
  • Yellow perch (Buckeye Lake, Indian Lake, Knox Lake): 30 a day, statewide rule
  • Bluegill / sunfish, carp, gar, bullhead: not on the statewide limits table, and none of the 11 lakes has a site-specific rule for them
