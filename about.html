About & disclaimer

Follow along Facebook Instagram X

The Night Bite is a personal, unofficial Ohio night fishing hub with a night-dock look, starting in central Ohio and covering 49 waters. Species pages, water maps, a trip log, and gear for each one; pick a water with Choose a lake at the top of any page. Night is especially useful for catfish (channel and flathead, plus blues at Hoover); it is not the best approach for every species.

Not official

The Night Bite is unofficial. It is not affiliated with the Ohio Department of Natural Resources (ODNR), the City of Columbus, the City of Mansfield, the Muskingum Watershed Conservancy District (MWCD), the Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission, USGS, the US Army Corps of Engineers, or the National Weather Service (NWS). Verify rules, hours and conditions before you go.

Hub scope

This hub covers 49 Ohio waters. Hoover Reservoir is the default. Public access names only: no secret GPS, no private property tips. Each water keeps its own boating and safety notes on Water, gear, and species pages.

All 49 waters

East and Southeast Ohio. Seneca Lake, Dillon Lake, Lake Logan, Salt Fork Lake, Burr Oak Lake, Dow Lake, Tappan Lake, Piedmont Lake and Clendening Lake.

Northeast Ohio. Mosquito Creek Lake, Berlin Lake, West Branch Reservoir, Pymatuning Lake, Lake Milton, Leesville Lake, LaDue Reservoir, Portage Lakes, Mogadore Reservoir, Nimisila Reservoir, Atwood Lake, Beach City Lake, Wingfoot Lake, East Branch Reservoir, Guilford Lake, Highlandtown Lake, Punderson Lake and Wellington Upground Reservoir.

Southwest Ohio. Caesar Creek Lake, East Fork Lake, C.J. Brown Reservoir, Rocky Fork Lake, Paint Creek Lake, Cowan Lake and Acton Lake.

Where the numbers come from

  • USGS: lake level and, where posted, water temperature come from USGS gauges. All gauge readings are provisional.
  • USACE: lake levels and, where posted, outflow water temperature come from US Army Corps of Engineers gauges. Outflow is labelled outflow, never as surface temperature, and never counts toward the bite rating.
  • ODNR: fishing maps, stocking and regulations come from ODNR. Contours are for reference, not for navigation.
  • NWS: forecast, barometer and alerts for each lake come from the National Weather Service.
  • MWCD: winter drawdown dates and levels for MWCD waters are on each of those pages.
  • Official city and park listings: ramp names, hours and local boating rules come from official city, park and marina pages, linked on the Water pages.

  • Madison Lake, Knox Lake and Clear Fork Reservoir: no public USGS or Corps lake level or water temperature gauge, so neither is shown.
  • Seneca Lake: USGS gauge 03141000 for lake level only. No public water temperature gauge, so none is shown.

  • Lake Logan, Salt Fork Lake and Dow Lake: no public USGS or Corps lake level or water temperature gauge, so neither is shown.
  • Mosquito Creek Lake, Berlin Lake, West Branch Reservoir, Lake Milton and Leesville Lake: USGS lake level gauges 03095000, 03090000, 03092450, 03091000 and 03120000. Berlin and Leesville compare with the Corps summer pool, since their ODNR maps use an older datum. No public water temperature gauge, so none is shown.
  • Pymatuning Lake: USGS gauge 03100500 hasn’t reported since June 4, 2026, so no level is shown as current. No public water temperature gauge.
  • Grand Lake St. Marys: USGS lake gauge 403204084342601 for level only. No public temperature gauge on this water, so none is shown.
  • Caesar Creek Lake: USGS lake gauge 03242340 for level. No live water temperature; the Corps posts a temperature profile, named with its date (latest Oct. 19, 2026) and never shown as live.
  • East Fork Lake: USGS lake gauge 03247040 for level, and the Corps’ outflow water temperature every 15 minutes, labelled outflow. No lake surface temperature is posted, and the outflow reading never counts toward the bite rating.
  • C.J. Brown Reservoir: USGS lake gauge 03268090 for level. No live water temperature; the Corps posts a temperature profile, named with its date (latest Oct. 7, 2026) and never shown as live.
  • Paint Creek Lake: US Army Corps of Engineers lake level gauge, and the Corps’ hourly outflow water temperature, labelled outflow. No lake surface temperature is posted, and the outflow reading never counts toward the bite rating.
  • Lake Loramie: USGS lake gauge 03261800 for level only (NAVD88; the ODNR map names no datum, so the pool difference is approximate). No public temperature gauge on this water, so none is shown.

  • Tappan, Piedmont, Clendening and Atwood lakes: USGS lake gauges 03128000, 03125500, 03126500 and 03121000 for level only. No public temperature gauge on these waters, so none is shown.

Safety

Fishing after dark can be dangerous. Storms, lightning, heat, cold, slick ramps, and low visibility are real risks. Turn back when weather turns. Use lights, tell someone your plan, and wear a life jacket when appropriate. Hoover, Alum Creek, O’Shaughnessy, Griggs and Clear Fork are drinking-water reservoirs. At every lake, pack out trash and follow posted rules.

License

An Ohio fishing license is required for anglers 16 and older (confirm current rules before you go). Buy and manage licenses through official ODNR / HuntFish OH channels, not through this site.

ODNR licenses / HuntFish OH · mention the HuntFish OH app for phone-side regs checks.

Regs reminders

The current 2026–27 rules are on the species and water pages. ODNR is the authority. Confirm on HuntFish OH before you keep fish. The official text is in ODNR Fishing Regulations 2026–27 (verify current).

Coming March 1, 2027: the Ohio Wildlife Council voted on Oct. 28, 2026 to drop the site-specific bass rules, and those waters go to the statewide 5 combined, 12″ min. The same vote removes Hoover’s crappie daily limit and 9″ minimum; from March 1, 2027 Hoover follows the statewide rule, no daily limit and no minimum (ODNR news release). Until then, the 2026–27 rules are the ones in force.

Statewide and site-specific reminders

Plain-language reminders from the statewide inland pattern in ODNR Fishing Regulations 2026–27 (verify current). Confirm on HuntFish OH before you keep fish. Some waters differ (e.g. the crappie 9″ rule at 22 of the 49 lakes, an 18″ bass minimum at Knox Lake, a 15″ bass minimum at Seneca Lake and Cowan Lake, bass slots at Salt Fork, Burr Oak, Dow, Paint Creek, Acton, Highlandtown, Wingfoot, Guilford and Long Lake (Portage Lakes), a 15″ walleye minimum at Mosquito Creek, Berlin, West Branch, Lake Milton, C.J. Brown and LaDue, and Pymatuning’s own Ohio and Pennsylvania rules).

  • Channel cat: under 28″ no daily limit; 28″+ daily 1
  • Blue cat: under 35″ no daily limit; 35″+ daily 1
  • Flathead: under 35″ no daily limit; 35″+ daily 1
  • Largemouth / smallmouth / spotted: 5 combined, 12″ min; at Knox Lake 5 combined, 18″ min; at Seneca Lake 5 combined, 15″ min; at Salt Fork 4 a day, 2 under 15″ and 2 15″ or longer; at Burr Oak and Dow 5 combined, none between 12″ and 15″; at Paint Creek, Acton and Highlandtown 4 a day, 2 under 15″ and 2 15″ or longer; at Cowan Lake 5 combined, 15″ min; at Long Lake in the Portage Lakes, Wingfoot and Guilford 3 a day, none from 14″ to 20″ and only 1 at 20″ or longer (site-specific rules). The Ohio Wildlife Council voted on Oct. 28, 2026 to drop all of these bass rules; from March 1, 2027 those waters go to the statewide 5 combined, 12″ min (ODNR news release)
  • Striped / hybrid striped / white bass: 30 combined; no more than 4 over 15″
  • Paddlefish: none may be taken within 2,000 ft of the Deer Creek dam, or in Caesar Creek Lake, East Fork Lake or Paint Creek Lake or within 2,000 ft of any of those lakes and dams (site-specific rules)
  • Walleye / sauger / saugeye: 6 combined; 15″ minimum at Mosquito Creek Lake, Berlin Lake, West Branch Reservoir, Lake Milton, C.J. Brown Reservoir and LaDue Reservoir (site-specific rule); no minimum at the other lakes except Pymatuning (below)
  • Crappie at 22 of the 49 lakes: 30 a day, black and white combined, 9″ minimum (site-specific rule for Hoover, Alum Creek, Buckeye Lake, O’Shaughnessy, Indian, Delaware, Deer Creek, Seneca, Dillon, Salt Fork, Mosquito Creek, Lake Milton, Grand Lake St. Marys, Caesar Creek, East Fork, Rocky Fork, Paint Creek, Lake Loramie, Tappan, Piedmont, Pleasant Hill and the Portage Lakes’ East, North, Turkeyfoot and West reservoirs). Griggs, Madison, Knox, Clear Fork, Lake Logan, Burr Oak, Dow, Berlin, West Branch, Leesville, C.J. Brown, LaDue, Mogadore, Nimisila, Cowan, Acton, Clendening, Atwood, Charles Mill, Beach City, Wingfoot, East Branch, Guilford, Highlandtown, Punderson and Wellington Upground are not on that list, so crappie there have no daily limit or minimum. The Ohio Wildlife Council voted on Oct. 28, 2026 to remove Hoover’s crappie daily limit and 9″ minimum; from March 1, 2027 Hoover follows the statewide rule, no daily limit and no minimum (ODNR news release). The other waters on that list keep 30 a day, 9″
  • Muskellunge (Alum Creek, Clear Fork, Salt Fork, Lake Logan, West Branch, Lake Milton, Leesville, Caesar Creek, C.J. Brown, Piedmont): 1 a day, statewide rule; no minimum length listed statewide
  • Yellow perch (Buckeye Lake, Indian Lake, Knox Lake, Seneca Lake, Salt Fork, Mosquito Creek, West Branch, Lake Milton, Leesville, Grand Lake St. Marys, Caesar Creek, Rocky Fork, LaDue, Tappan, Atwood, Pleasant Hill): 30 a day, statewide rule
  • Bluegill / sunfish, carp, gar, bullhead: not on the statewide limits table, and none of the other 42 lakes has a site-specific rule for them
  • Pymatuning Lake (Ohio and Pennsylvania rules): crappie 20 a day, 9″ minimum; bass 5 a day, 12″ minimum; muskie 2 a day, 30″ minimum; walleye 6 a day, 15″ minimum; all other fish no limit. Up to 3 lines with 3 hooks each, and a Pennsylvania license is needed on the Pennsylvania shore or any island
