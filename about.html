About & disclaimer

The Night Bite is a personal, unofficial Ohio night fishing hub with a night-dock look, starting in central Ohio, covering 23 lakes: Hoover Reservoir, Alum Creek Lake, Buckeye Lake, O’Shaughnessy Reservoir, Griggs Reservoir, Indian Lake, Delaware Lake, Deer Creek Lake, Madison Lake, Knox Lake, Clear Fork Reservoir, Seneca Lake, Dillon Lake, Lake Logan, Salt Fork Lake, Burr Oak Lake, Dow Lake, Mosquito Creek Lake, Berlin Lake, West Branch Reservoir, Pymatuning Lake, Lake Milton and Leesville Lake. Species pages, water maps, trip log, and gear for each one; pick a lake with Choose a lake at the top of any page. Night is especially useful for catfish (channel and flathead, plus blues at Hoover); it is not the best approach for every species.

Hub scope

  • 23 Ohio lakes: Hoover Reservoir, Alum Creek Lake, Buckeye Lake, O’Shaughnessy Reservoir, Griggs Reservoir, Indian Lake, Delaware Lake, Deer Creek Lake, Madison Lake, Knox Lake, Clear Fork Reservoir, Seneca Lake, Dillon Lake, Lake Logan, Salt Fork Lake, Burr Oak Lake, Dow Lake, Mosquito Creek Lake, Berlin Lake, West Branch Reservoir, Pymatuning Lake, Lake Milton and Leesville Lake. Hoover is the default.
  • Public access names only: no secret GPS, no private property tips.
  • Each lake keeps its own boating and safety notes on Water, gear, and species pages: 10 hp at Hoover, unlimited horsepower on Alum Creek’s south main lake with no wake on the north end, no wake within 300 ft of any land mass at Buckeye Lake, unlimited horsepower with a 40 mph limit by day, 10 mph from sunset to sunrise and no jet skis at the City’s O’Shaughnessy and Griggs reservoirs, no horsepower limit at Indian Lake, Delaware Lake or Deer Creek Lake, electric motors only at Madison Lake, 10 hp at Knox Lake (larger motors at no wake), idle speed, no wake on all of Clear Fork Reservoir with no horsepower limit (City of Mansfield), a 399 hp limit at Seneca Lake, unlimited horsepower at Dillon Lake, Salt Fork Lake, Mosquito Creek Lake, West Branch Reservoir and Lake Milton, no horsepower limit by day and 10 mph at night at Berlin Lake, 20 hp at Pymatuning Lake, and 10 hp at Lake Logan, Burr Oak Lake, Dow Lake and Leesville Lake.

Where the numbers come from

  • Hoover: USGS gauge 03228400 for pool level and water temperature.
  • Alum Creek: US Army Corps of Engineers lake level gauge. No public water temperature gauge, so none is shown.
  • Buckeye Lake: USGS lake gauge 395540082291600 for level only. No public water temperature gauge, so none is shown.
  • O’Shaughnessy: USGS gauge 03220500 for level only. The City says reservoir temperatures are not monitored, so none is shown.
  • Griggs: USGS gauge 03221500 for pool level and water temperature.
  • Indian Lake: USGS gauge 03260500 for lake level and water temperature.
  • Delaware Lake: US Army Corps of Engineers lake level gauge. No public water temperature gauge, so none is shown.
  • Deer Creek Lake: US Army Corps of Engineers lake level gauge. No public water temperature gauge (the Corps posts an outflow temperature only), so none is shown.
  • Madison Lake, Knox Lake and Clear Fork Reservoir: no public USGS or Corps lake level or water temperature gauge, so neither is shown.
  • Seneca Lake: USGS gauge 03141000 for lake level only. No public water temperature gauge, so none is shown.
  • Dillon Lake and Burr Oak Lake: US Army Corps of Engineers lake level gauges (Burr Oak is held by the Corps’ Tom Jenkins Dam). No public water temperature gauge, so none is shown.
  • Lake Logan, Salt Fork Lake and Dow Lake: no public USGS or Corps lake level or water temperature gauge, so neither is shown.
  • Mosquito Creek Lake, Berlin Lake, West Branch Reservoir, Lake Milton and Leesville Lake: USGS lake level gauges 03095000, 03090000, 03092450, 03091000 and 03120000. Berlin and Leesville compare with the Corps summer pool, since their ODNR maps use an older datum. No public water temperature gauge, so none is shown.
  • Pymatuning Lake: USGS gauge 03100500 hasn’t reported since June 4, 2026, so no level is shown as current. No public water temperature gauge.
  • Forecast, barometer and alerts for each lake come from the National Weather Service. All gauge readings are provisional.

Safety

Fishing after dark can be dangerous. Storms, lightning, heat, cold, slick ramps, and low visibility are real risks. Turn back when weather turns. Use lights, tell someone your plan, and wear a life jacket when appropriate. Hoover, Alum Creek, O’Shaughnessy, Griggs and Clear Fork are drinking-water reservoirs. At every lake, pack out trash and follow posted rules.

License

An Ohio fishing license is required for anglers 16 and older (confirm current rules before you go). Buy and manage licenses through official ODNR / HuntFish OH channels, not through this site.

ODNR licenses / HuntFish OH · mention the HuntFish OH app for phone-side regs checks.

Regs reminders (verify 2026–27)

Plain-language reminders from the statewide inland pattern in ODNR Fishing Regulations 2026–27 (verify current). Confirm on HuntFish OH before you keep fish. Some waters differ (e.g. the crappie 9″ rule at 12 of the 23 lakes, an 18″ bass minimum at Knox Lake, a 15″ bass minimum at Seneca Lake, bass slots at Salt Fork, Burr Oak and Dow, a 15″ walleye minimum at Mosquito Creek, Berlin, West Branch and Lake Milton, and Pymatuning’s own Ohio and Pennsylvania rules).

  • Channel cat: under 28″ no daily limit; 28″+ daily 1
  • Blue cat: under 35″ no daily limit; 35″+ daily 1
  • Flathead: under 35″ no daily limit; 35″+ daily 1
  • Largemouth / smallmouth / spotted: 5 combined, 12″ min; at Knox Lake 5 combined, 18″ min; at Seneca Lake 5 combined, 15″ min; at Salt Fork 4 a day, 2 under 15″ and 2 15″ or longer; at Burr Oak and Dow 5 combined, none between 12″ and 15″ (site-specific rules)
  • Striped / hybrid striped / white bass: 30 combined; no more than 4 over 15″
  • Paddlefish: none may be taken within 2,000 ft of the Deer Creek dam (site-specific rule)
  • Walleye / sauger / saugeye: 6 combined; 15″ minimum at Mosquito Creek Lake, Berlin Lake, West Branch Reservoir and Lake Milton (site-specific rule); no minimum at the other lakes except Pymatuning (below)
  • Crappie at 12 of the 23 lakes: 30 a day, black and white combined, 9″ minimum (site-specific rule for Hoover, Alum Creek, Buckeye Lake, O’Shaughnessy, Indian, Delaware, Deer Creek, Seneca, Dillon, Salt Fork, Mosquito Creek and Lake Milton). Griggs, Madison, Knox, Clear Fork, Lake Logan, Burr Oak, Dow, Berlin, West Branch and Leesville are not on that list, so crappie there have no daily limit or minimum. ODNR has proposed dropping the 9″ minimum at Hoover from March 1, 2027
  • Muskellunge (Alum Creek, Clear Fork, Salt Fork, Lake Logan, West Branch, Lake Milton, Leesville): 1 a day, statewide rule; no minimum length listed statewide
  • Yellow perch (Buckeye Lake, Indian Lake, Knox Lake, Seneca Lake, Salt Fork, Mosquito Creek, West Branch, Lake Milton, Leesville): 30 a day, statewide rule
  • Bluegill / sunfish, carp, gar, bullhead: not on the statewide limits table, and none of the other 22 lakes has a site-specific rule for them
  • Pymatuning Lake (Ohio and Pennsylvania rules): crappie 20 a day, 9″ minimum; bass 5 a day, 12″ minimum; muskie 2 a day, 30″ minimum; walleye 6 a day, 15″ minimum; all other fish no limit. Up to 3 lines with 3 hooks each, and a Pennsylvania license is needed on the Pennsylvania shore or any island
