Whisperwood Vale

A cozy woodland fishing game with ponds, mist, and a journal that stays on your device. Not the real lake. Not Hoover. Fiction beside the Night Bite notebook.

PLAYABLE IN THE BROWSER

Cast, wait for the bobber, keep a fish journal. Progress saves locally, no account needed.
