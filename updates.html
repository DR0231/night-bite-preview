What’s new

New features, improvements and fixes on The Night Bite, newest first.

  1. October 27, 2026 New

    Share tonight, Remind me, and season pages

    • Share tonight makes a picture card with the wind, weather, sunset, water temp and what's biting, ready to send to your fishing buddies.
    • Remind me sets a reminder for any time of day, from a time wheel or the Sunset button, in your calendar or on your phone.
    • Species knows your lake: it shows the fish in that lake first, marks what's biting there this month, and lists ramps and access.
    • Water adds dock season notes for each lake, and the new Ice season page covers ice safety and what still bites in winter.
    • Home
    • Species
    • Dock season
    • Ice season

  2. October 27, 2026 New

    Lake home pages and a faster Home

    • Alum Creek Lake and Buckeye Lake have their own home pages.
    • The live lake level and tonight's bite now sit at the top of Home.
    • Home and Species no longer jump around while they load.
    • After an update, a page reloads only when it is out of date.
    • Hoover home
    • Alum Creek home
    • Buckeye home

  3. October 24, 2026 Improved

    Trip reports for every lake

    • The trip report form asks which lake you fished: Hoover Reservoir, Alum Creek Lake or Buckeye Lake.
    • Its species list covers all three lakes, including muskellunge, yellow perch and bullhead.
    • The Log page's report link fills in the lake you picked.
    • Log

  4. October 24, 2026 New

    Alum Creek Lake and Buckeye Lake

    • Pick Hoover Reservoir, Alum Creek Lake or Buckeye Lake with the new lake picker.
    • Each lake has its own map, lake level gauge (USGS, or the US Army Corps of Engineers at Alum Creek), species and tournaments.
    • New species pages for muskellunge, yellow perch and bullhead.
    • Pages pick up each new release on their own, even if your phone saved an older copy.
    • Home
    • Water
    • Species
    • Tournaments

  5. October 24, 2026 Improved

    More weather for tonight

    • An hourly forecast strip through 2 AM.
    • Tonight's bite: a rule of thumb rating from the forecast.
    • Last light and moonset, the 3 hour pressure change, feels like, dew point and wind on the water.
    • A storm heads up when thunderstorms are in the forecast.
    • Home

  6. October 22, 2026 New

    Tonight at Hoover, weather alerts and the lake map

    • Tonight at Hoover on Home: barometer with its 12 hour trend, wind with gusts, and tonight's moon phase.
    • National Weather Service watches and warnings show in a banner at the top of every page.
    • Home adds the 7 day lake level trend and a map of Hoover, and readings refresh on their own.
    • Species shows what's biting this month.
    • Works offline, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.
    • Home
    • Species
    • Add to home screen

  7. October 20, 2026 Improved

    A cleaner look

    • A new Home with a moonlit horizon and a chart of Hoover on the lake level card.
    • One set of pixel icons, with a fish for each species.
    • Gear has pixel rig diagrams with numbered parts and step by step how to cards.
    • About lists Hoover's fishing limits from the 2026 to 2027 ODNR regulations.
