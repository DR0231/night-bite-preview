Ice season

Winter on Hoover Reservoir: ODNR’s ice safety rules, who to call, and what this site’s notes say still bites in the cold months.

No ice is safe ice. That is ODNR’s rule, and this page follows it. The Night Bite never reports ice conditions or thickness and never calls ice safe. Judge it yourself, every trip, and stay off when in doubt.

Before you step out

From ODNR’s ice fishing safety page:

  • Fish with a partner, or where other anglers are around.
  • Tell someone exactly where you are going, where you will park and when you will be back.
  • Wear a life vest, carry ice awls, and keep your phone in a plastic bag.
  • Stay away from feeder streams, springs, bridge pilings, docks and dam structures, where ice is usually very thin.
  • If someone else falls in: reach with a stick or rod, throw a rope or flotation, row or push a boat, go call for help.

ODNR’s thickness guidelines are minimums for new, clear, solid ice only, and it says many things other than thickness can make ice unsafe. Read them there; this site doesn’t repeat them as a go or no go.

Sources: ODNR, Ice Fishing Safety; ODNR, Ice Thickness Guidelines. Checked Oct. 27, 2026.

On Hoover

Columbus runs Hoover for drinking water, and the city describes it as “subject to significant water level fluctuation.” Reservoir rules are in Columbus City Code Chapters 919 and 921. Check them before going out on the ice.

  • Columbus Watershed Management: 614-645-1721

Source: City of Columbus, Watershed Management. Checked Oct. 27, 2026.

The US Army Corps of Engineers manages Alum Creek’s water level. Call the state park office before going out on the ice.

  • Alum Creek State Park office: 740-548-4631 (Mon to Fri, 8 am to 4:30 pm)

Source: ODNR, Alum Creek State Park. Checked Oct. 27, 2026.

ODNR names Buckeye Lake one of the best places for saugeye through the ice, because it is shallow and generally freezes quickly. Ice that forms fast is still not safe ice. Call the state park office before going out.

  • Buckeye Lake State Park office: 740-527-4218 (Mon to Thu, 8 am to 5 pm)

Sources: ODNR, Ice Fishing Safety; ODNR, Buckeye Lake State Park. Checked Oct. 27, 2026.

Columbus runs O’Shaughnessy Reservoir for drinking water. Reservoir rules are in Columbus City Code Chapters 919 and 921. Check them before going out on the ice.

  • Columbus Watershed Management: 614-645-1721

Sources: City of Columbus, Watershed Management; Columbus Recreation and Parks, Marinas & Docks. Checked Oct. 29, 2026.

Indian Lake is an ODNR state park lake. Read ODNR’s ice safety advice and call the state park office before going out on the ice.

  • Indian Lake State Park office: 937-843-2717

Sources: ODNR, Ice Fishing Safety; ODNR, Indian Lake State Park. Checked Oct. 29, 2026.

The US Army Corps of Engineers manages Delaware Lake’s water level for flood control, and ODNR lists a 5 ft winter drawdown. Call the state park office before going out on the ice.

  • Delaware State Park office: 740-363-4561

Sources: ODNR, Delaware State Park; ODNR, Winter Drawdowns. Checked Oct. 29, 2026.

The US Army Corps of Engineers manages Deer Creek Lake’s level for flood control, and ODNR lists a 14 ft fall drawdown, so the ice can sit over moving, falling water. ODNR lists ice fishing at the park under the proper conditions. Call the park office before going out on the ice.

  • Deer Creek State Park office: 740-869-3124

Sources: ODNR, Deer Creek State Park; ODNR, Winter Drawdowns at Ohio State Park Lakes.

No public gauge posts Madison Lake’s level, and ODNR posts no ice report for it. Madison Lake is run from the Deer Creek State Park office; call before going out on the ice.

  • Park office (at Deer Creek State Park): 740-869-3124

Sources: ODNR, Madison Lake State Park.

No public gauge posts Knox Lake’s level, and ODNR posts no ice report for it. Call ODNR before going out on the ice.

  • ODNR Knox Lake Fishing Area: 614-644-3925

Sources: ODNR, Knox Lake Fishing Area.

The City of Mansfield runs Clear Fork Reservoir as a water supply lake and posts no ice report. No public gauge posts the level. Call the park police before going out on the ice.

  • Clearfork Park police and information: 419-884-1408

Sources: City of Mansfield, Clearfork Reservoir Park; ODNR, Clear Fork Reservoir Fishing Area.

Ice fishing rules, a reminder

ODNR: “In Ohio, anglers can have up to six tip-ups going at one time and each must be labeled with the owner’s name and address.” Hole size, shelters and the rest are in the current Ohio fishing regulations. Verify them before you go.

Sources: ODNR, Ice Fishing Safety; Ohio Fishing Regulations 2026-27 (PDF). ODNR Wildlife: 1-800-WILDLIFE.
