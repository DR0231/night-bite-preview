The Log

Cream-paper trip cards from Hoover nights. Private notes stay in this browser. Community trips load from the notebook JSON. Entries marked Example are demos, not live bite reports.

How submit works
  • Community list reads data/community-log.json in the repo.
  • Submit by mailto draft or GitHub issue markdown. No account needed to read.
  • Text trip notes only for now: no photos, likes, Instagram, or logins.
  • Public ramp names only. No secret GPS, no private property.
