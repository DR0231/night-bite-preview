Black crappie

Solid Hoover panfish: spring banks and brush, then deeper wood and channel edges.

Alum Creek's coves north of Cheshire Road are the spring crappie spot.

Buckeye Lake warms first in central Ohio, so spring crappie come early and shallow.

ODNR fish surveys record black crappie at O’Shaughnessy. ODNR posts no where notes for them there, so the rest of this page is Hoover notes.

ODNR fish surveys record black crappie at Griggs. ODNR posts no where notes for them there, so the rest of this page is Hoover notes.

ODNR reports lots of black and white crappie over 9 inches, with a real chance at 14 to 16 inch fish.

Delaware’s best known fish, per ODNR: both crappie species reproduce well and many are over 9 inches.

ODNR lists crappie among Deer Creek Lake’s excellent catches, and its fish surveys record black and white crappie.

ODNR lists crappie among Madison Lake’s plentiful catches, and its fish surveys record black and white crappie.

ODNR lists black and white crappie at Knox Lake, and its fish surveys record both.

ODNR fish surveys record black and white crappie at Clear Fork.

ODNR fish surveys record black and white crappie at Seneca Lake, and ODNR posts a Seneca crappie survey.

ODNR fish surveys record black and white crappie at Dillon Lake, and ODNR’s park page lists crappie as plentiful.

ODNR’s Lake Logan State Park page lists crappie as abundant. It doesn’t say which crappie.

ODNR fish surveys record black and white crappie at Salt Fork Lake, and ODNR posts a Salt Fork crappie survey.

ODNR says black crappie are present at Burr Oak in lower numbers than white crappie.

Not a regular at Dow Lake: ODNR doesn't list it there. The rest of this page is Hoover notes.

Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring: shallow banks and cover, peak bank fishing.
  • Summer: deeper brush, docks (public), channel swings.
  • Fall: follow bait into creeks and arms.
  • Winter: deeper schools, so vertical presentations from a boat.

Time of day

Dawn/dusk excellent. Night under lights (public) can be fun in summer. Midday summer often deeper.

Where on Hoover

  • Upper arms / Oxbow for spring bank.

Limits reminder, verify

Hoover has its own crappie rule in the 2026–27 booklet: 30 a day, 9″ minimum. ODNR has proposed dropping the 9″ minimum from March 1, 2027, so check the current rules before you keep fish.
