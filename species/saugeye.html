Species · Saugeye

Saugeye

Stocked saugeye have rebounded since about 2017, a real Hoover target for low-light trolling and casting.

A main target at Alum Creek, best at dusk and after dark on rock.

Buckeye's signature fish, stocked by ODNR.

Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring: shallow low-light bites as water warms.
  • Summer: dawn/dusk and night; deeper by day.
  • Fall: strong feed along breaks.
  • Winter: slower, deeper presentations.

Time of day

Night and twilight are strengths: soft plastics and crankbaits along breaks. Daytime possible in stained water or deep.

Where on Hoover

  • Channel edges and breaks on the ODNR 894 map (reference).
  • Bank: cast along drops near Walnut / public riprap where safe.
