Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring–fall shallow feeding on flats.
  • Summer mornings/evenings on banks.
  • Winter slower.

Time of day

Daytime sight-fishing on flats; night possible with baited areas on public shoreline.

Where on Hoover

  • Public mud flats and upper arms from shore.
  • Quiet banks away from busy ramps and picnic areas.
  • No private property baiting.
  • Mud flats and upper arms reachable by ≤10 hp / kayak.
  • Idle speed within 100 ft of shore (no swimming anywhere on Hoover).
  • No private property baiting.

Gear

Sized for bank reality and ≤10 hp boats. Hoover is a drinking-water reservoir.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Alum Creek allows unlimited horsepower on the south main lake, so expect wakes there; the north end is no wake.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Buckeye Lake is shallow with stumps: idle near shore (no wake within 300 ft).

Sized for bank reality and small boats. O’Shaughnessy allows unlimited horsepower (40 mph limit by day, 10 mph after sunset), so expect wakes by day; idle within 100 ft of shore.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Griggs allows unlimited horsepower (40 mph limit by day, 10 mph after sunset) on a narrow lake; idle within 100 ft of shore.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Indian Lake is shallow with islands and channels: idle in the 300 ft no wake zone.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Delaware allows unlimited horsepower, and the level moves with flood control.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Deer Creek Lake allows unlimited horsepower, and the level moves with flood control.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Madison Lake is electric motors only.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Knox Lake has a 10 hp limit.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Clear Fork is no wake on the whole lake.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Seneca Lake has a 399 hp limit and long open water.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Dillon Lake allows unlimited horsepower, and the level moves with flood control.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Lake Loramie is no wake except the west end, so speed does not matter much.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Cowan Lake has a 10 hp limit and is drawn down 13.5 ft for dam work through 2028.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Acton Lake keeps boats over 10 hp at no wake, so a small motor does the job.

Sized for bank reality and small boats. Tappan Lake allows up to 399 hp; the main lake is long and open.
