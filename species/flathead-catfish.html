Flathead catfish

Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring: move shallow around cover as water warms.
  • Summer nights: prime time, when big flats hunt after dark.
  • Fall: continue near structure until water cools hard.
  • Winter: deep holes; slower bite.

Time of day

Night is classic flathead time. Quiet banks and lighted docks (public only) can draw baitfish. Stay legal and respectful.

Where on Hoover

  • Timber, rock, and channel bends on the ODNR contour map (public water only).
  • Bank stretches near Walnut Road and quieter upper arms.
  • Timber, rock, and channel bends on the ODNR contour map (public water only).
  • Boat: edges of the old channel and cover (10 hp / 10 mph rules).

Bait & presentation

  • Live bait where legal, or very fresh cut bait.
  • Present near cover; don’t snag private property or docks.
  • Patience: flatheads often hit later in the night.

Notes
