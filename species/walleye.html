Walleye

Stocked in 2019 as part of a study against saugeye. They’re here through stocking, so don’t assume a huge wild population.

Season

  • Similar low-light windows to saugeye when present.
  • Spring and fall often better than bright summer midday.
  • Night along breaks if bait and fish show.
