Species · Walleye

Walleye

Stocked in 2019 as part of a study against saugeye. They’re here through stocking, so don’t assume a huge wild population.

Present at Alum Creek (ODNR species list).

Not a regular at Buckeye Lake: ODNR doesn't list it there. Buckeye’s stocked cousin is the saugeye, a walleye and sauger hybrid. The rest of this page is Hoover notes.

ODNR stocked 288,728 walleye fingerlings in Hoover in 2019. Stocking & surveys
