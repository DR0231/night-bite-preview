Species · Blue catfish

Blue catfish

Trophy fishery. Blues were first stocked in 2011 and have become a Hoover highlight.

Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Ice-out / spring: fish transitioning; look for warming banks and channel edges.
  • Summer: night cut-bait sessions in deeper basins and channel.
  • Fall: strong feed windows as water cools.
  • Winter: deep, slower presentations. Check ice and safety separately (this site is night-boat/bank focused).

Time of day

Night and low-light favor big blues on cut bait. Daytime can work in deeper water when boat traffic allows.

Where on Hoover

  • Public ramps: Walnut, Redbank, Oxbow. Check hours.
  • Main basin / dam end deeper water (see the ODNR 894-ft contours, reference only).
  • Old Big Walnut channel edges.

Gear

Sized for bank reality and ≤10 hp boats. Hoover is a drinking-water reservoir.

  • Heavier gear for trophies: 20–40 lb line, stout rod, quality drag.
  • 10 hp boat helps cover channel; bank anglers can still score near deeper public access.
  • Strong hooks; measure carefully if near the 35″ trophy line.

Bait & presentation

  • Fresh cut bait (shad, skipjack if legal where obtained, bluegill where legal as bait; confirm regs).
  • Large chunks on the bottom; patience over constant moving.

Notes

ODNR Hoover Fishing Area: blues first stocked in 2011, and some older fish already top 30 lb. In 2019 Hoover produced 41 Fish Ohio blues (35″+), more than any other Ohio water. Always measure before keeping a big fish.
