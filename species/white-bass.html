Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring: run up Big Walnut Creek (public access only; respect closures and property).
  • Summer: open-water schools on shad. Watch birds and surface activity.
  • Fall: continue schooling until cool-down.
  • Winter: deeper, less of a night focus.

Time of day

Dawn/dusk and whenever shad are busting. Night can produce under lights or moon when schools are up.

Where on Hoover

  • Big Walnut Creek spring run, from public banks and bridges only.
  • Windward main-lake points when schools push bait shoreward.
  • Watch for surface busts near public shore; stay off private docks.
  • Open-water schools on shad. Follow birds and the graph (≤10 hp).
  • Main-lake points and creek mouths when bait stacks.
  • Spring: idle toward public creek access; respect closures/property.
