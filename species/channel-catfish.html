Channel catfish

Hoover’s workhorse night fish, reliable on cut bait and crawlers from the bank or a 10 hp boat.

Present at Alum Creek (ODNR species list).

Abundant at Buckeye Lake, best on the flats after dark.

Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Ice-out / early spring: slow but starting. Mud-line banks warm first.
  • Spring: pre-spawn and spawn push fish shallow; night bank bites pick up.
  • Summer: classic night fishery. After dark is often the best window.
  • Fall: feed-up before cool-down; cut bait still works.
  • Winter: deep and slow; daytime can outfish night when water is cold.

Time of day

Night is the strength for channels here: lantern hours after the boat traffic dies. Dawn/dusk also produce, especially in spring and fall.

Where on Hoover

  • Public flats near Walnut Road lower ramp (flat vs nearby drop).
  • Public shoreline only, no private docks.
