Species · White crappie

White crappie

Shares Hoover with black crappie, with the same spring bank push and deeper summer brush.

Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring shallow spawn/pre-spawn on banks.
  • Summer deeper cover and channel-related brush.
  • Fall creek arms.
  • Winter deeper vertical bite.

Time of day

Same as black crappie: low light and spring days on the bank, night lights optional.

Where on Hoover

  • Public banks in Oxbow / upper ends in spring.
  • Brush piles and timber (public water).
  • See Water maps + ODNR PDF for contours.

Limits reminder, verify

Same Hoover rule as black crappie: 30 a day combined, 9″ minimum (2026–27). ODNR has proposed dropping the 9″ minimum from March 1, 2027.

Gear

Sized for bank reality and ≤10 hp boats. Hoover is a drinking-water reservoir.

  • Light spinning / bobber or jig setups.
  • Bank or 10 hp boat. Small jigs travel light.

Bait & presentation

  • Minnows, tube jigs, small plastics.
  • Match depth to season: shallow in spring, deeper in summer.

Notes

Treat limits as black+white combined unless HuntFish says otherwise. Measure for 9″ at Hoover.
