Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring: warming shallow bays wake them up first.
  • Summer: steady nights in shallow, muddy water.
  • Fall: good until the water cools.
  • Winter: slow.

Time of day

After dark is best. Overcast evenings work too.

Where on Buckeye Lake

  • Shallow, soft bottom bays and around docks, a crawler on the bottom.
  • Public ramps, parks, shore and the Mud Island pier only. Pins on the water maps.
  • Shallow bays and flats near shore after dark.
  • Idle within 300 ft of shore; watch for stumps in the dark.

Gear

  • Light to medium rod, 8 to 12 lb line.
  • Small circle hooks, a slip sinker, a rod holder and a headlamp.
  • Mind the sharp pectoral and dorsal spines when unhooking.

Bait & presentation

  • Nightcrawlers, the classic.
  • Chicken liver or cut bait.
  • Fish it still on the bottom.

Notes

Black, brown and yellow bullhead are all small catfish. Good fun for kids on a summer night.

Water & maps

Contours, pool correction, public ramps.
