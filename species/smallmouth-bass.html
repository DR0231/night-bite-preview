Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring: rocky banks as water warms.
  • Summer: deeper rock and points; early/late best.
  • Fall: chase bait on main-lake structure.
  • Winter: deep and slow.

Time of day

Low light and wind-blown rock. Night can work near rocky public banks; day bite often better in cooler months.
