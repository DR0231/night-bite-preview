Hybrid striped bass (wiper)

Possible in many Ohio reservoirs and share the striped/white combined limit. White bass are confirmed at Hoover; treat hybrids as “possible / verify reports,” not a guarantee.

Not a regular at Alum Creek: ODNR doesn't list it there. The rest of this page is Hoover notes.

ODNR stocks hybrid striped bass in Buckeye Lake (every year from 2021 to 2025 in its stocking records), and its surveys record them. ODNR posts no where notes for them there, so the rest of this page is Hoover notes.

ODNR stocks hybrid striped bass in O’Shaughnessy, and its fish surveys record them. ODNR posts no size or catch numbers for them here.

ODNR says Griggs has a strong population of large hybrids, often over 20 inches. In summer heat, watch for schools chasing shad and gulls diving.

Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring–fall: open water and current areas when bait is present.
  • Often mixed in reports with white bass schools, so identify carefully.
  • Winter: deeper if present.

Time of day

Low light and bait blitzes, the same windows as white bass when schools are up.

Where on Hoover

  • Public points and creek banks shared with white bass. No secret pins.
  • Verify recent angler reports; don’t assume stocking density.
  • Open basin and points where shad school (boat advantage, ≤10 hp).
