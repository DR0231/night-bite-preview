Season

  • Spring/early summer: beds in shallows, classic bank fun.
  • Summer: weed edges and docks (public).
  • Fall: still catchable shallow/mid.
  • Winter: slower, deeper.

Time of day

Daytime beds in late spring; early/late in summer heat. Fine night fishing under lights for fun.
