Largemouth bass

More abundant than smallmouth on Hoover. Docks, weeds, and shoreline cover (public water).

Present at Alum Creek (ODNR species list).

Present at Buckeye Lake (ODNR species list).

ODNR fish surveys record largemouth bass at O’Shaughnessy. ODNR posts no where notes for them there, so the rest of this page is Hoover notes.

ODNR fish surveys record largemouth bass at Griggs. ODNR posts no where notes for them there, so the rest of this page is Hoover notes.

Largemouth do well in the shallow water and vegetation, ODNR says, in good numbers though most are under 15 inches.

A popular bass and tournament lake. ODNR’s largemouth numbers have trended down but are still good, mostly 12 to 15 inches.

ODNR lists largemouth bass among Deer Creek Lake’s excellent catches.

ODNR lists bass among Madison Lake’s plentiful catches, and its fish surveys record largemouth.

Historically one of Ohio’s best largemouth lakes, ODNR says: 51% of bass collected were over 15 inches. 18″ minimum, 5 a day (2026–27 rules); from March 1, 2027 the statewide 12″ minimum applies.

Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring: pre-spawn and spawn in shallows. Be mindful of bed ethics and regs.
  • Summer: early/late and night around cover; midday deeper or shade.
  • Fall: chase bait schools and creek arms.
  • Winter: slow; deeper structure.

Time of day

Dawn, dusk, and night around lighted public structure. Midday summer often needs shade or deeper edges.

Where on Hoover

  • Creek arms and quieter banks.
