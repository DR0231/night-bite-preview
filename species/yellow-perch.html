Species · Yellow perch

Yellow perch

Listed by ODNR at Buckeye. Rock and weed edges with minnows or crawlers near the bottom; less steady than crappie.

Not a regular at Hoover: ODNR doesn't list it there. This page covers Buckeye Lake.

Not a regular at Alum Creek: ODNR doesn't list it there. This page covers Buckeye Lake.

Season Where Limits Gear Bait

Season

  • Spring: pre-spawn fish move shallow early, since Buckeye warms first.
  • Summer: scattered; mixed in with bluegill on weed edges.
  • Fall: one of the steadier times on rock and weed edges.
  • Winter: a classic ice target when the ice is safe.

Time of day

A daytime fish. Midday is fine; they go quiet after dark.

Where on Buckeye

  • Rocky public shore and riprap, minnows on a slip float or a light jig.
  • Public ramps, parks, shore and the Mud Island pier only. Pins on the water maps.
  • Weed edges and harder bottom off the old towpath; drift until you find a school.
  • Contours on the ODNR fishing map; idle within 300 ft of shore.

Gear

  • Light spinning rod, 4 to 6 lb line.
  • Small hooks (size 6 to 8), split shot and a slip float.
  • A bucket or stringer only if you plan to keep fish.

Bait & presentation

  • Live minnows, the top choice.
  • Pieces of nightcrawler or waxworms.
  • Small jigs tipped with a minnow, worked near the bottom.

Notes

Perch school by size; if you only catch small ones, move.

Water & maps

Contours, pool correction, public ramps.
