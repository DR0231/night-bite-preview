Species

Hoover hub for all listed species: cats, bass, panfish, saugeye/walleye, and a few incidentals. Alum Creek hub: saugeye, muskie, smallmouth and largemouth, crappie and the rest of the lake's listed species. Buckeye Lake hub: saugeye, crappie, panfish and channel cats in a shallow, fast warming lake. O’Shaughnessy hub: stocked hybrid striped bass, saugeye and blue cats, plus crappie, bass, bluegill, white bass and channel cats from ODNR surveys. Griggs hub: large hybrid striped bass, per ODNR, plus saugeye, crappie, bass, bluegill, white bass and channel cats from ODNR surveys. Indian Lake hub: saugeye, big crappie, bluegill, largemouth, white bass and channel and flathead cats in a shallow lake. Delaware Lake hub: crappie first, plus largemouth and smallmouth, channel cats, bluegill and white bass. Deer Creek Lake hub: spring saugeye below the dam, plus crappie, largemouth and smallmouth, channel cats, bluegill and white bass. Madison Lake hub: bass, crappie, bluegill, channel cats and bullhead on a small, electric only lake. Knox Lake hub: big largemouth under an 18″ minimum (statewide 12″ from March 1, 2027), trophy flatheads, crappie, bluegill, channel cats and perch. Clear Fork hub: stocked muskie, plus largemouth, crappie and channel cats from ODNR surveys. Seneca Lake hub: stocked saugeye and blue cats, plus crappie, largemouth and smallmouth, bluegill, white bass and perch. Dillon Lake hub: stocked saugeye, hybrid stripers and blue cats, plus crappie, bass, channel cats and bluegill. Lake Logan hub: stocked saugeye and channel cats, plus largemouth, bluegill, crappie and muskie. Salt Fork Lake hub: stocked muskie and saugeye, plus bass, crappie, bluegill, white bass, perch and walleye. Burr Oak Lake hub: largemouth first, plus crappie, bluegill, channel and blue cats and the odd flathead. Dow Lake hub: stocked saugeye, plus largemouth, white crappie and channel cats. Mosquito Creek Lake hub: stocked walleye under a 15″ minimum, plus crappie, perch, largemouth, channel cats and bluegill. Berlin Lake hub: stocked walleye under a 15″ minimum, plus white bass, smallmouth and largemouth, crappie and channel cats. West Branch hub: stocked muskie, walleye and blue cats, plus bass, crappie, white bass, perch, bluegill and bullhead. Pymatuning hub: walleye, muskie, perch and crappie under the Ohio–Pennsylvania rules, plus bass, white bass, channel cats and bluegill. Lake Milton hub: stocked walleye and muskie, plus white bass, smallmouth and largemouth, crappie, channel cats, bluegill, perch and bullhead. Leesville Lake hub: stocked muskie, saugeye and channel cats, plus hybrid stripers, bass, crappie, bluegill and perch. Grand Lake St. Marys hub: crappie under a 30 fish, 9″ rule, stocked perch and saugeye, plus walleye, largemouth, channel and flathead cats, bluegill, bullhead and carp. Caesar Creek Lake hub: stocked muskie, saugeye and blue cats, plus channel cats, largemouth and smallmouth bass, white bass, crappie under a 30 fish, 9″ rule and yellow perch. East Fork Lake hub: stocked hybrid stripers, saugeye and blue cats, plus largemouth and smallmouth bass, crappie under a 30 fish, 9″ rule, bluegill and white bass. C.J. Brown Reservoir hub: stocked walleye and muskie, plus largemouth and smallmouth bass, white bass, crappie, bluegill and catfish. Rocky Fork Lake hub: stocked saugeye, plus largemouth, crappie under a 30 fish, 9″ rule, white bass, channel and flathead cats, bluegill, perch and big carp. LaDue Reservoir hub: stocked walleye and channel cats, plus largemouth and smallmouth bass, crappie, bluegill and yellow perch. Paint Creek Lake hub: stocked saugeye, plus bass under a 4 fish split rule (statewide from March 1, 2027), crappie under a 30 fish, 9″ rule, white bass, channel cats and bluegill. Portage Lakes hub: largemouth, crappie under a 30 fish, 9″ rule, bluegill, channel cats stocked in Turkeyfoot, bullhead and carp. Mogadore Reservoir hub: largemouth, crappie, bluegill and stocked channel cats. Lake Loramie hub: stocked saugeye, plus crappie under a 30 fish, 9″ rule, largemouth, bluegill, channel cats, bullhead and carp. Nimisila Reservoir hub: stocked channel cats, plus largemouth, crappie, bluegill, walleye and saugeye. Cowan Lake hub: largemouth under a 15″ rule, stocked saugeye, crappie, bluegill, and channel and flathead cats. Drawn down 13.5 ft through 2028. Acton Lake hub: stocked saugeye, bass under a 4 fish split rule, white and black crappie, and bluegill. Tappan Lake hub: stocked saugeye, plus largemouth and smallmouth bass, crappie under a 30 fish, 9″ rule, white bass, channel cats and bluegill. Piedmont Lake hub: stocked muskie and saugeye, plus walleye, largemouth and smallmouth bass, crappie under a 30 fish, 9″ rule, white bass and bluegill. Atwood Lake hub: stocked saugeye and blue catfish, plus channel cats, largemouth and smallmouth bass, white bass, white and black crappie and yellow perch. Charles Mill Lake hub: stocked saugeye and hybrid striped bass, plus largemouth bass, white and black crappie and channel cats. Pleasant Hill Lake hub: stocked saugeye, plus largemouth and smallmouth bass, crappie under a 30 fish, 9″ rule, white bass, bluegill, channel cats and bullhead.     Channel catfish

    Hoover’s workhorse night fish, reliable on cut bait and crawlers from the bank or a 10 hp boat.

    Present at Alum Creek. Night flats and creek channel edges with cut bait or crawlers; not the headline fish here.

    Abundant at Buckeye Lake. Shallow flats near the deeper holes after dark, cut bait or crawlers.

    Recorded in ODNR fish surveys at O’Shaughnessy.

    Recorded in ODNR fish surveys at Griggs.

    About half the channel cats in ODNR’s hoop nets were 8 years old, most of them 18 to 20 inches.

    Largemouth bass

    More abundant than smallmouth on Hoover. Docks, weeds, and shoreline cover (public water).
