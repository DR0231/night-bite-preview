Privacy

The Night Bite and Whisperwood Vale have no accounts, no cookies, no ads, and no analytics or tracking scripts. Most of what you do here stays in your own browser.

Last updated October 22, 2026.

What stays in your browser

A few features save small notes in your browser’s localStorage so they are still there next visit. They are not sent to The Night Bite. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes them.

  • The Log: your personal trip entries (nightbite-log-v1) and a flag for the example trips (nightbite-samples-seeded-v2).
  • Gear: your packing checklist ticks (nightbite-pack-v1) and the bank / boat toggle (nightbite-mode-v1).
  • Offline readings: the last pool reading, 7 day pool trend and weather forecast your browser fetched (nb-usgs-last, nb-trend-last, nb-cond-last), so Home can show them, marked “Offline”, when you have no signal.
  • Add to home screen tip: whether you closed it (nb-install-tip-v1).
  • Whisperwood Vale: your game save (whisperwood-save-v1 and related keys), journal and settings. If you export or import a save file, that file stays wherever you put it.

The site also keeps copies of its own pages, styles, fonts and pictures in your browser’s cache (a service worker), so they open at the lake without signal. Whisperwood Vale is never saved this way. Clearing this site’s data removes the copies too.

Don’t type secrets, card numbers, or home addresses into trip notes.

Whisperwood feedback (Google Forms)

The Whisperwood Vale feedback button opens a Google Form. It asks for a rating, optional notes, and an optional email if you’d like a reply. No sign-in is required. Google handles the form under the Google Privacy Policy, and responses go to The Night Bite’s Google account.

Trip and tournament submissions

  • Email draft: the trip and tournament forms on Log only build a draft in your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • GitHub issue: issues on GitHub are public and handled under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visit for security purposes (GitHub Pages data collection, GitHub Privacy Statement). The Night Bite doesn’t receive those logs.

Live water and weather readings

The Home and Water pages ask for the latest gauge reading for the lake you’ve picked, straight from your browser: USGS (waterservices.usgs.gov) for Hoover and Buckeye Lake, and the US Army Corps of Engineers CWMS data service (cwms-data.usace.army.mil) for Alum Creek. Opening the lake picker on any page asks the same services for the latest reading at all three lakes. Each service sees those requests like any other website visit. No personal data is sent with them. The Home page asks the National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) for the forecast at the selected lake, and every page asks it for active alerts there, the same way.

Fonts and other sites

Fonts are served from this site, not from a font service. Links to ODNR, USGS, the US Army Corps of Engineers, Columbus, Google, and GitHub take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.
